# Radbro Survivors art review queue

Keep each candidate in a separate Radclaw run. Keep the source and hashes.
Use one brief for each comparison. Do not mix art approval with engine checks.

## 1. Player direction

Compare the current native 64-pixel paperdoll with the released Radcat proof.
Use the same arena, camera scale, lighting, and 20-second route.
Capture idle, walk, left mirror, contact, XP pickup, and an upgrade pause.
Show the original NFT portrait beside each partial outfit preset.
List matched and missing traits. Do not call a partial outfit exact.

Candidate A: Keep the current front three-quarter paperdoll and mirror it.
Candidate B: Author a true side view on the same native 64-pixel grid.
Candidate C: Author front, rear, left, and right views on one shared rig.

Use the current 64 by 64 canvas and floor anchor (32,60).
Keep idle, walk, jog, and wave timing from the selected pack.
Keep shirts split into far sleeve, torso, and near sleeve planes.
Export editable Aseprite source, untrimmed PNG atlases, pack metadata, and file hashes.

Decision criteria: character identity, silhouette at game scale, animation readability, equipment alignment, direction clarity, and cost for one new outfit.
Approve a direction before making the full cast.

## 2. Enemy family

Replace the original geometric sentries with three original Radclaw enemies.
Compare a small robot, a strange creature, and a corrupted toy.
Use 48 by 48 frames with a documented foot anchor.
Require idle, walk, hurt, and dissolve clips.
Use the same test speed and 20-enemy crowd for every candidate.
Keep enemy shapes distinct from the hero and green XP.
Provide editable source, timing, provenance, and explicit release permission.

## 3. Weapon and feedback

Make a separate orbiting blaster. Keep its pivot and muzzle position in metadata.
Compare an improvised scrap blaster and a clean toy-like device.
Require idle, fire flash, projectile, impact, XP shard, and heal effects.
The weapon must aim without replacing the hero's arm animation.
Check left and right travel with each outfit plane.

## 4. Arena and sound

Compare a sunlit abandoned court with a neon maintenance yard.
Provide a seamless 32-pixel floor tile, edge strips, and non-blocking props.
Keep contact areas clear. Avoid floor details that look like XP or projectiles.
Request distinct fire, hurt, XP, upgrade, defeat, and win sounds.
Record source and redistribution terms for every file.

## Approval record

For each review, save a contact sheet, a playable capture, and technical checks.
Record the selected direction and rejected options.
Keep unselected drafts out of public packs.
Only add a released asset version after approval and license notices exist.
