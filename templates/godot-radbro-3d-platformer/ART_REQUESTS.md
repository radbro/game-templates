# Art direction review queue

Use the Radclaw workflow before adding new art.
Keep source references, editable masters, runtime files, permissions and hashes.
Make local comparison sheets. Do not replace a released pack until art approval is recorded.

| Asset | Current proof | Compare next | Required engine proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Player | Published Radcat Motion v2 | Radbro toy figure; low-poly explorer; retain Radcat | Idle, jog, takeoff, air pose and landing; full bone and morph tracks |
| Islands | Kenney CC0 platforms | Floating garden; rooftop city; arcade toy course | Readable edges, collision fit, repeatable scale, camera contrast |
| Falling island | Kenney yellow platform | Cracked stone; warning-lit metal; soft cloud | Clear warning before fall; distinguish from safe islands |
| Coin | Kenney CC0 coin | Radbro badge; energy orb; equipment token | Clear silhouette at camera distance; unambiguous collection |
| Finish | Kenney CC0 flag | Radbro pennant; portal; delivery dock | Clear locked/unlocked states; visible goal at last island |
| Effects and sound | Kenney CC0 sound | Compare jump, coin and fall sets | Comfortable browser mix; optional mute; no licensed music without notice |

Keep alternate art in ignored Radclaw run folders.
Render each option at the same game camera and lighting.
Show an idle frame, a jump frame, a movement clip and a full course screenshot.
Judge identity, silhouette, motion, environment contrast and small-screen readability separately.
New Radbro 3D review candidates need their own release record and public permission notice.
