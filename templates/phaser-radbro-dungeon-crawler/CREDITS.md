## Dungeon art

Tiles, lizards, treasure chests, knife, and heart icons: 0x72, Dungeon Tileset II.
Source: https://0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal.
Terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Verified on 2026-10-18. The author page states that the tileset can be used for any purpose under CC0.
The same page lists its asset license as Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal.
The included PNGs are the original Ourcade atlas or extracted tile bytes.
Their pinned upstream Git LFS hashes were checked before copying.
The original level JSON and source license are included unchanged.

The original starter also credits Ansimuz's Legend of Faune.
This port excludes that character art. It does not use that pack.
No audio or third-party font files are included.

## Radcat

Character sprites: Radbro.
Source: https://radbro.gg/shared-assets/radcat-motion-v2/radcat-motion-v2-source.zip
Credit link: https://radbro.fun/assets
Keep `assets/radcat-motion-v2/LICENSE.txt` and `SPRITE-PERMISSION.txt` with the sprites.
These are separate asset permissions. MIT does not replace them.
Source PNGs and animation JSON stay unchanged.
The project uses six 320 by 320 frames each for idle and walk.
The idle frame duration is 300 ms. The walk frame duration is 120 ms.
